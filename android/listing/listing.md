## Full description (≤4000)

Study for the NREMT EMT cognitive exam the way it is built.

EMT Prep 2026 follows the five content domains of the current NREMT EMT test plan — Scene Size-up and Safety, Primary Assessment, Secondary Assessment, Patient Treatment and Transport, and Operations — in the same proportions the exam uses. Primary Assessment carries the most weight on the exam, so it carries the most weight here.

ADAPTIVE PRACTICE EXAM
Questions get harder or easier based on your answers, the same idea as the computer adaptive test you will take: 70 to 120 questions, 2-hour limit, no feedback until the end and no going back. The exam stops once your result is clear, then shows how you did in each domain and every question you missed.

250 SCENARIO QUESTIONS
Every question puts you on a call: the patient, the scene, the vital signs. You decide what to do, the way the real exam asks. Each answer explains why it is right, why each wrong choice is wrong, and the one principle to remember. Missed a principle? Practice it again right away with related questions.

ITEM TYPES YOU WILL SEE ON TEST DAY
Multiple choice, multiple response ("select two" or "select three"), build-list ordering, and classification tables. Scoring is all-or-nothing, as on the real exam.

LISTEN MODE
Study hands-free on the drive or a walk. The app reads each question and its choices, gives you time to think, then reads the answer and why. Skills checklists can talk you through each step too.

MEMORY CARDS
51 cards for what you must know cold: AVPU, GCS, OPQRST, SAMPLE, START and JumpSTART, CPR numbers, oxygen flow rates, the rule of nines and more, each with a way to remember it.

SPACED REVIEW
Missed questions come back in about ten minutes. Questions you get right return after 1, 3, 7, 16 and 35 days, so your study time goes where it is needed.

KNOW YOUR WEAK SPOT
The Progress screen ranks domains by exam weight times the share you are missing, so a moderate gap in a heavily weighted domain outranks a big gap in a small one. Your weakest topics are listed by name.

SKILLS PRACTICE
Step-by-step practice checklists for nine psychomotor stations: trauma and medical patient assessment, BVM ventilation, cardiac arrest and AED, oxygen by non-rebreather, bleeding control and shock, spinal motion restriction, long-bone and joint immobilization. Each has a critical-failure list, a timer and a log of your runs.

CURRENT GUIDELINES
Resuscitation questions follow the 2025 American Heart Association Guidelines for CPR and ECC, including the updated choking sequence and infant compression technique. Every question includes an explanation and its reference.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone and the app works without a connection.
