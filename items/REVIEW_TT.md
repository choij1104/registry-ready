## What was verified against sources
- **CPAP**: county EMS protocols (example: ICPHD Policy 7040, EMT-authorized). Indication: awake, alert, able to follow commands, with distress or hypoxia. Contraindications: inability to protect the airway or altered mental status, hypotension, pneumothorax, vomiting, respiratory failure. Supports TT-001/002/003/013.
- **Nitroglycerin and PDE-5 inhibitors**: AHA/ACC NSTE-ACS guideline as quoted by EBM Consult. Contraindicated within 24 h of sildenafil or vardenafil and within 48 h of tadalafil. Supports TT-008 (tadalafil the previous night) and TT-009 (sildenafil 6 h ago).
- **Opioid emergency**: 2025 AHA Part 10 (Special Circumstances) and the 2025 Highlights. Give naloxone for respiratory arrest from suspected opioid overdose. It is given in addition to standard resuscitation and must not delay CPR or breaths. Supports TT-015.
- **SMR**: ACS-COT/ACEP/NAEMSP joint position statement (NAEMSP page). Blunt-trauma indications are altered LOC or intoxication, midline pain or tenderness, focal neuro signs, deformity, and distracting injury. The statement says: "There is no role for SMR in penetrating trauma." Supports TT-030/031.
- **Traction splint**: EMS standard-of-care document (Milwaukee County). Indication is a mid-thigh injury. Contraindications are pelvic fracture and distal lower-extremity fracture. Supports TT-032.
- **TBI ventilation**: BTF prehospital TBI guidelines, 3rd edition, as summarized by ACEP Now. Avoid hyperventilation. Use normal rates of about 10/min targeting EtCO2 35–45. Hyperventilation is only an EtCO2-targeted measure for active herniation. This led to the TT-029 rewrite; TT-028 is confirmed.
- **Neonatal resuscitation**: 2025 AHA/AAP Part 5. Routine suctioning is not recommended (Class 3). Give PPV within 60 s if gasping or apneic, or if HR stays below 100. Start compressions if HR stays below 60 despite 30 s of ventilation that inflates the lungs. A 3:1 ratio is reasonable. Supports TT-041 as revised.
- **OB complications**: San Diego County EMS S-133 (BLS). Prolapsed cord: elevate the hips, push the presenting part off the cord, cover the cord with moist gauze, transport. Nuchal cord: slip it over the head, or clamp and cut if tight. Breech head not delivering in 1–2 min: create an airway with a gloved hand and transport. PPH: vigorous fundal massage, baby to breast, O2, keep warm. Supports TT-037/038/039/040.
- **Heat stroke**: NATA position statement ("cool first, transport second"; cold-water immersion cools fastest). TT-046 tests recognition by mental status and is consistent with this source. No item in this file keys cooling priority.
- **Hypothermia**: 2025 AHA Part 10, which covers removing wet clothing and passive rewarming. The AHA text read does not address gentle handling, which is standard EMT teaching (risk of VF in a cold heart). TT-047 is consistent.
- **Drowning**: 2025 AHA Part 10. Trained rescuers give breaths. Compression-only CPR is only for rescuers unwilling or unable to give breaths. Supports TT-048.
- **Field triage**: 2021 National Guideline handout (ACS). Red criteria include bleeding needing a tourniquet or packing, motor GCS below 6, suspected pelvic fracture, and penetrating torso injury. Yellow criteria include anticoagulant use and a low-level fall with head impact at 65 or older. Supports TT-051, and TT-044 as revised.
- **Snakebite**: national and regional guidance found in search. No tourniquet, ice, or incision and suction. Remove constricting items, immobilize, transport. The PMC consensus paper (PMC3550186) was behind a captcha and could not be read. TT-049 matches standard EMT curriculum.

Sources that could not be read: the PMC prehospital TBI executive summary (permission prompt timed out), the PMC snakebite consensus (captcha), and the NASEMSO Model Guidelines v3 PDF (host blocked by the proxy). Secondary or regional sources were used for those points instead.
