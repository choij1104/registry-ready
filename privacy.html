EMT Prep 2026 — Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026 · HAKOYA LLC

EMT Prep 2026 is a study app for the NREMT EMT exam, published by HAKOYA LLC. This policy covers the Android app and the web version.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not send your answers or any other information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your study progress (which questions you answered, your review schedule, practice exam results and skills practice runs) is saved only in the app's storage on your device so you can pick up where you left off. Uninstalling the app or using "Erase my data" in the app removes it.

Permissions

The Android app requests no permissions beyond what its built-in web view needs to run. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for EMT certification and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
